Privacy Policy

PrintLoad (Android) · Last updated: August 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PrintLoad handles information when you use our Android app on Google Play.

1. Information We Collect

PrintPal does not require an account and does not collect your name, email, or phone number for standard use.

  • Local content: Images and PDFs you select stay on your device.
  • Permissions: Camera, storage, and network permissions are used only for scan/print features.
  • Purchases: Handled by Google Play. We only keep a local premium flag on the device.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide scanning, PDF tools, and printing
  • Save default print settings on device
  • Enable premium features after purchase

3. Data Storage & Sharing

Files are not uploaded to our servers. We do not sell your data to third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

Google Play Billing may process payments under Google’s privacy policy.

5. Children’s Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13.
